# Glycothon interest form: questions for Google Forms

Create a Google Form, then in the Responses tab choose "Link to Sheets". Set the form to collect no email addresses unless you need them, and paste the form link into index.html where it says https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe6MR2kKdvO_49ZDJJxWNYyQuHVdzFbnAKuZph1yPCMi2Aohw/viewform.

Intro text for the form:
Glycothon is a proposed CASP inspired hackathon for glycoscience methods. The goal is a shared pool of tools and standards. It is not meant to create or deposit basic glycoscience research. This form only records your interest, nothing commits you.

1. Full name (short answer, required)
2. Email (short answer, required)
3. Institution and country (short answer, required)
4. Career stage (multiple choice, required): Student, Postdoc, Principal investigator, Industry, Other
5. How would you like to take part? (checkboxes, required): Team member (hands on work), Jury member (evaluating submissions), Both, Just keep me informed
   Note for the form: the idea is that students and postdocs form the teams and principal investigators form the jury.
6. Your main background (checkboxes): Computational or bioinformatics, Mass spectrometry, Other experimental glycoscience, Structural biology or modeling, Data standards and databases, Other
7. Which challenge interests you most? (checkboxes): Structure prediction, Matching glycan and carrier, Structure function relationships, Biological function of glycan decorations, Standards and nomenclature, Not sure yet
8. Could you provide sampled or dummy data as references and for experimentation? (multiple choice): Yes, Maybe, No
9. If yes or maybe, what kind of data? (paragraph): for example LC MS/MS spectra, glycan structures, glycoproteomics, binding data. Please do not send unpublished results.
10. Tools, databases or workflows you could contribute (paragraph, optional)
11. May we list your name on the website as a participant or jury member? (multiple choice): Yes, No
12. Anything else? (paragraph, optional)
